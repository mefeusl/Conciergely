# Pre-Mortem

Imagine the trade has failed. Why?

## Procedure
1. Name three distinct failure paths. Mix types:
   - **Idea is wrong** (the thesis, earnings, sector)
   - **Idea is right, timing/price is wrong** (stopped out then recovers, entered after the move)
   - **Outside shock** (macro, currency, regulation, liquidity, gap over the stop)
2. For each: an early-warning sign the user can watch, and what they would do (pre-agreed, e.g. exit at the stop, reduce size).
3. Ask the one question that matters most: "What would make you exit even if the price has not hit your stop?"
4. Offer to record it in the journal with a confidence percentage.

## Style
Three short bullets, not an essay. Plain WhatsApp text. No scare tactics, no reassurance.
